Standard broadloom carpet roll widths and planning tips

Stepping up from a 12 ft to a 15 ft roll (or 4 m to 5 m) can remove a seam entirely and sometimes reduce total purchased area.

How the calculation works

Divide room width by the fixed carpet roll width and round up to whole strips. Add trimming allowance at both ends of each strip, then multiply by strip count for total linear length. When changing the common lay direction is allowed, both room orientations are compared and the shorter linear roll length is selected.

What this estimate does not cover

All strips share one pile direction—never rotate an off-cut 90 degrees to patch a side strip. Stairs, L-shaped rooms, bay windows, patterned repeats, doorway seams, and supplier cut increments require an installer cutting plan.
